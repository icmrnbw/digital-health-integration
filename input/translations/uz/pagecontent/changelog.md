### Versiya 0.7.0

DHP Integrations IG ning birinchi nashr etilgan relizi. Raqamlash 0.7.0 dan boshlanadi, chunki qo'llanma versiyasi u asoslangan [UZ Core](https://dhp.uz/fhir/core/) relizi bilan mos kelishi kerak. Taqqoslash uchun oldingi reliz yo'q, shuning uchun ushbu yozuv qo'llanma tarkibini tavsiflaydi.

#### Klinik hujjatlar

Sog'liqni saqlash vazirligining to'rtta shakli FHIR hujjatlari sifatida profillangan: [011-shakl, gemodializ seansi](StructureDefinition-form-011-hemodialysis-composition.html), [066-shakl, statsionardan chiqqan bemorning statistik kartasi](StructureDefinition-form-066-hospital-discharge-composition.html), [066-1-shakl, psixiatriya (narkologiya) statsionaridan chiqqan bemorning statistik kartasi](StructureDefinition-form-066-1-psychiatric-discharge-composition.html) va [130-shakl, laboratoriya tekshiruvlari natijalari](StructureDefinition-form-130-laboratory-test-results-composition.html). Har biri uchun namunaviy hujjat-Bundle va qog'oz shaklning har bir maydonini FHIR elementiga bog'laydigan moslashtirish sahifasi mavjud: [011-shakl](form-011-mapping.html), [066-shakl](form-066-mapping.html), [066-1-shakl](form-066-1-mapping.html) va [130-shakl](form-130-mapping.html).

[Hujjat toifalari va identifikatorlari](document-categories.html) sahifasi hujjatlar qanday tasniflanishini - Sog'liqni saqlash vazirligi shakl raqamlarini o'z ichiga olgan [hujjat toifasi](CodeSystem-document-category-cs.html) kod tizimi orqali - hamda rasmiy [shakl raqamlari](NamingSystem-doc-form-number.html) va [shablon raqamlari](NamingSystem-doc-template-number.html) alohida nusxa identifikatorlari bilan birga qanday qayd etilishini tushuntiradi. [Klinik hujjat hayotiy sikli](document-statuses.html) sahifasi qo'llanadigan Composition holatlarini va ular orasidagi o'tishlarni bayon qiladi.

[UZ Core Encounter 066](StructureDefinition-uz-core-encounter-066.html) profili bemor kasalxonaga yotqizilishidan oldin qayerdan kelganini qayd etuvchi [qabul manbai](StructureDefinition-admission-origin.html) kengaytmasini qo'shadi. [Tashxis roli](CodeSystem-diagnosis-role-integration.html) kod tizimi ushbu shakllar ajratadigan rollarni qamrab oladi: yo'llagan muassasa tashxisi va qabuldagi tashxisdan tortib asosiy, raqobatlashuvchi, yondosh va fon tashxislari orqali o'lim bilan bog'liq rollargacha.

#### Sil kasalligi (DHIS)

DHIS dagi sil kasalligi ma'lumotlari hujjatlar sifatida emas, alohida resurslar sifatida almashiladi va [sil kasalligi integratsiyasi](dhis-tuberculosis.html) sahifasida maydonma-maydon moslashtirilgan. [DHIS Condition](StructureDefinition-dhis-condition.html) tashxisni saqlaydi, [DHIS Episode of Care](StructureDefinition-dhis-episode-of-care.html) bemorning tashxisi, davolanishi va kuzatuvini birlashtiradi, [DHIS Specimen](StructureDefinition-dhis-specimen.html) laboratoriya tekshiruvi uchun olingan materialni tavsiflaydi, [DHIS Observation](StructureDefinition-dhis-observation.html) esa tekshiruv natijalarini saqlaydi va [balg'am surtmasi mikroskopiyasi](StructureDefinition-dhis-observation-microscopy.html) (bakterial yuklama darajasi bilan), [ko'krak qafasi rentgenogrammasi natijalari](StructureDefinition-dhis-observation-xray.html) va [OIV ko-infeksiyasi natijalari](StructureDefinition-dhis-observation-hiv.html) profillariga ixtisoslashadi.

Manba tizim ishlatadigan kodlar kod tizimlari sifatida nashr etilgan - [tashxislar](CodeSystem-dhis-condition-code-cs.html), [tekshiruv kodlari](CodeSystem-dhis-observation-code-cs.html), [komponentlar](CodeSystem-dhis-observation-component-code-cs.html), [natijalar](CodeSystem-dhis-observation-codeable-concept-cs.html), [usullar](CodeSystem-dhis-observation-method-cs.html), [namuna turlari](CodeSystem-specimen-type-cs.html), [namuna xususiyatlari](CodeSystem-specimen-feature-type-cs.html) va [dorilarga sezgirlik](CodeSystem-tb-drug-sensitivity-cs.html) - va qabul qiluvchilar mahalliy kodlarga bog'liq bo'lib qolmasligi uchun har biri standartga moslashtirilgan: [tashxislar](ConceptMap-dhis-tuberculosis-to-snomed.html), [natijalar](ConceptMap-dhis-observation-result-to-snomed.html), [namuna turlari](ConceptMap-specimen-type-conceptmap.html) va [namuna xususiyatlari](ConceptMap-specimen-feature-type-to-snomed.html) SNOMED CT ga, [diagnostik tekshiruvlar](ConceptMap-dhis-observation-tuberculosis-code.html) va [dorilarga sezgirlik komponentlari](ConceptMap-dhis-observation-component-to-loinc.html) LOINC ga.

#### Bachadon bo'yni va ko'krak bezi saratoni skriningi

[Skrining integratsiyasi](cervical-breast-cancer-screening.html) sahifasi bachadon bo'yni saratoni skriningi va ko'krak bezi saratonini erta aniqlash tizimini FHIR ga moslashtiradi. [Screening Patient](StructureDefinition-screening-patient.html) va [Screening Practitioner](StructureDefinition-screening-practitioner.html) UZ Core profillari asosida qurilgan; [Screening ServiceRequest](StructureDefinition-screening-service-request.html) - tekshiruvlarga yo'llanmalarni; [Screening Observation](StructureDefinition-screening-observation.html) - natijalar va biometrik o'lchovlarni; [Screening DiagnosticReport](StructureDefinition-screening-diagnostic-report.html) - mammografiya, sitologiya va kolposkopiya bo'yicha yakuniy xulosalarni; [Screening DocumentReference](StructureDefinition-screening-document-reference.html) - rozilik shakllari va bayonnomalarni.

Natijalar [BI-RADS](CodeSystem-screening-birads-cs.html), [bachadon bo'yni sitologiyasi](CodeSystem-screening-cervical-cytology-cs.html), [kolposkopiya](CodeSystem-screening-colposcopy-cs.html), [OPV](CodeSystem-screening-hpv-cs.html), [transformatsiya zonasi](CodeSystem-screening-transformation-zone-cs.html), [mammografiya topilmalari](CodeSystem-screening-breast-finding-cs.html), [ko'krak bezi zichligi](CodeSystem-screening-breast-form-cs.html), [tasvir sifati](CodeSystem-screening-image-quality-cs.html) va [ko'krak bezi kvadranti](CodeSystem-screening-breast-quadrant-cs.html) kodlari bilan kodlanadi; oxirgisi topilmani joylashtiruvchi [ko'krak bezi kvadranti kengaytmasi](StructureDefinition-breast-quadrant.html) orqali ishlatiladi. ConceptMap lar [ko'krak bezi topilmalari](ConceptMap-screening-breast-finding-to-snomed.html), [bachadon bo'yni sitologiyasi](ConceptMap-screening-cervical-cytology-to-snomed.html), [kolposkopiya](ConceptMap-screening-colposcopy-to-snomed.html) va [OPV](ConceptMap-screening-hpv-to-snomed.html) natijalarini SNOMED CT ga, dasturning o'z [mutaxassis rollarini](CodeSystem-screening-roles-cs.html) esa [milliy rollarga](ConceptMap-screening-roles-to-national.html) o'giradi.

#### Mehnatga layoqatsizlik varaqasi

[Mehnatga layoqatsizlik varaqasi](sick-leave.html) sahifasi varaqa qanday ifodalanishini tavsiflaydi. [Sick Leave CarePlan](StructureDefinition-sick-leave-careplan.html) - bu holatning o'zi, [Sick Leave Observation](StructureDefinition-sick-leave-observation.html) CarePlan ga sig'maydigan atributlarni saqlaydi, [Sick Leave RelatedPerson](StructureDefinition-sick-leave-related-person.html) esa o'zining [tug'ilgan sana kengaytmasi](StructureDefinition-relatedperson-birthdate.html) bilan oila a'zosiga qarash uchun berilgan varaqalarni qamrab oladi. Kengaytmalar [ish jarayoni holati](StructureDefinition-care-for-workflow-status.html) va uning [amal qilish davrlari bilan tarixini](StructureDefinition-care-for-status-history.html), [tashxisdan foydalanish turini](StructureDefinition-care-for-diagnosis-use.html) hamda varaqa kimga qarash uchun berilganini ko'rsatuvchi [bog'liq shaxsni](StructureDefinition-care-for-related-person.html) qayd etadi.

Yordamchi terminologiya varaqaning [toifasini](CodeSystem-sick-leave-category-cs.html) (oddiy, bolaga qarash uchun, ta'lim olayotgan shaxslar uchun, mastlik holatida berilgan), holat qayd etadigan [kuzatuv komponentlarini](CodeSystem-sick-leave-component-cs.html), nogironlik guruhini aniqlashni ham o'z ichiga olgan [xulosalarini](CodeSystem-conclusions-cs.html), holat [sababini](CodeSystem-care-plan-reason-cs.html), bemorning [shahar yoki qishloq](CodeSystem-care-plan-belonging-cs.html) aholisiga tegishliligini, holat o'tadigan [mahalliy holatlarni](CodeSystem-care-plan-status-local-cs.html), [rejim buzilishlarini](CodeSystem-regimen-violation-cs.html), [hodisa joyini](CodeSystem-place-of-incident-cs.html), [hodisa va murojaat orasidagi vaqt oralig'ini](CodeSystem-disease-injury-delay-cs.html), oila a'zosiga qarashda [qarindoshlik darajasini](CodeSystem-relation-degree-cs.html) va [mahalliy tashrif sinflarini](CodeSystem-encounter-class-local-cs.html) qamrab oladi. Varaqa raqamlari uchun alohida [nomlash tizimi](NamingSystem-sick-leave-number-system.html) mavjud.

#### Terminologiya

Kod tizimlarining to'qqiztasi qo'shimchalar (supplement) hisoblanadi: ular yangi tushunchalar kiritmaydi, balki boshqa joyda aniqlangan kodlarga o'zbekcha va ruscha nomlarni qo'shadi. Ular SNOMED CT ni [sil kasalligidagi lokalizatsiyalar](CodeSystem-dhis-body-site-cs.html), [skriningdagi lokalizatsiyalar](CodeSystem-screening-body-site-cs.html), [diagnostik muolajalar](CodeSystem-screening-diagnostic-procedure-cs.html), [skrining kuzatuv kodlari](CodeSystem-screening-observation-codes-cs.html), [kuzatuv turlari](CodeSystem-screening-observation-type-cs.html) va [ko'krak bezi topilmalari](CodeSystem-screening-snomed-breast-finding-cs.html) uchun; LOINC ni [laboratoriya tekshiruvlari](CodeSystem-screening-laboratory-cs.html) uchun; v3-ActCode ni [tashrif sinfi](CodeSystem-encounter-class-official-cs.html) uchun; FHIR so'rov holati kodlarini esa [parvarish rejasi holati](CodeSystem-care-plan-status-official-cs.html) uchun to'ldiradi.

SNOMED CT qo'shimchalari vaqtinchalik yechimdir. SNOMED CT ning o'zbek tiliga to'liq tarjimasi ustida ish olib borilmoqda va u tayyor bo'lgach, ushbu qo'shimchalar o'rniga o'sha tarjima qo'llaniladi.

Torroq ro'yxat bilan cheklanmagan tashxislar [to'liq XKT-10 qiymatlar to'plamiga](ValueSet-icd-10-vs.html) bog'langan. [Mahalliy kuzatuv kodi](CodeSystem-local-observation-code.html) tizimi hech bir standartda qamrab olinmagan yagona tushunchani - oldingi gospitalizatsiyalar sonini - saqlaydi.
